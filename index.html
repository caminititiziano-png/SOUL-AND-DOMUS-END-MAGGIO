Domus / The Property

One estate may become several private homes.

A large property can be too expensive for one buyer. When technically and legally feasible, subdivision can transform it into several identifiable private homes, each aligned with a different budget and life plan, without losing the charme of the original place.

The buyer does not buy a vague share. The buyer is intended to buy a real, private, identifiable home. Any common area, service or limitation must be explained before commitment.

Opportunity becomes value only when it can be reviewed and confirmed. Subdivision scenarios are always subject to municipal approval, technical feasibility, costs, timing and professional confirmation. In some feasibility models, a legal subdivision may indicate a value uplift around 10-20% or more, always as an indicative hypothesis to be reviewed and confirmed.

Protected Raw Acquisition

Entering before the final surface, after the serious checks.

In selected projects, a client may express interest before full renovation or finishing has started. This can create access to a more favorable indicative range, because the final price may change as documents, municipal steps, works and finishes are clarified. The numbers below are examples of how a future property presentation may explain entry range, not a public offer.

Protected Raw Acquisition does not mean buying an abandoned ruin without order. It means acquiring a verified portion after the legal, urban and structural review has been completed and explained, with the essential division logic, access, technical separation and base works defined before the client enters.

The starting price can include the protected portion together with the agreed raw-stage works: division works, main technical predispositions, plant routes, structural or municipal steps within the approved perimeter and the practical elements needed to make the future home readable. The property may remain intentionally unfinished inside, so the client saves against a fully finished delivery and keeps the freedom to complete the interior later.

This raw condition is designed to be intelligent, not speculative: it can lower the entry point, preserve transformation flexibility and create a more real starting value for personal use or for a future resale scenario. No resale result is guaranteed, but the client is not entering blindly: the base works included in the starting price are stated before the decision.

Before the purchase, even when the acquisition concerns a divided raw portion, the client can receive written finishing scenarios: what is already included in the raw-stage price, what remains to be completed later and what a finished version may cost under different interior directions, always working from the budget indication communicated by the client before the decision.

Questions We Prefer To Answer Clearly

Trust grows when the difficult questions are visible.

A serious acquisition path must make limits visible, not hide them. These answers are intentionally direct.

Are you a real estate portal?

No. We do not begin with a catalogue of properties. We begin with the buyer, then we review whether a property or opportunity is coherent with the person, budget, risk and lifestyle.

Do you sell property shares or fractional ownership?

No. The intention is a private, real and identifiable home. If a large property is subdivided, every scenario must be legally and technically confirmed before commitment.

Can you review a property I found myself?

Yes. This is one of the clearest ways to understand our method. You can send a link, location or documents and we can help identify missing questions and professional checks.

Does your Verified seal guarantee the property?

No. It means the available dossier has been organized and read through our internal document-review method. It does not replace legal, notarial, tax, technical or municipal confirmation.

Do you reimburse the client if something goes wrong?

Yes, if the client loses under a written Private Room protection. During the private conversations this depends on the specific case. Any refund, remedy or protection mechanism must be agreed in advance in writing, with clear scope, conditions and limits, signed by both parties. When a written remedy is part of the agreement, the client sees it clearly before moving forward.

Do you hold client funds or escrow money?

No purchase funds should be held by Soul & Domus. When useful, payment protection should be structured through notaries, banks or authorized instruments.

Can subdivision increase value?

It can create opportunity only when it is legal, technically feasible and documented. In some feasibility examples, the potential uplift may be modeled around 10-20% or more, but every estimate is case by case and never an automatic promise.

What happens if a property is not suitable?

We say so. A beautiful property can still be a poor decision if documents, access, costs, timing or municipal feasibility are not clear enough.

What is Domus Care?

Domus Care is the post-acquisition layer: local coordination, arrival care, house preparation, trusted suppliers and practical support. Services are subject to area, availability and approved estimates.
