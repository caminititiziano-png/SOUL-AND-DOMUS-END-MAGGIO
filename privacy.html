Privacy Policy

This page explains how Soul & Domus may process information submitted through this website. This first public version of the website does not create a client account or private database through the contact form. The form sends the request to hello@soulanddomus.com through the email service configured for the website.

Data You May Send

If you contact us, you may choose to send your name, email address, country, telephone or WhatsApp number, indicative budget, preferred region, family size, timing, property link and any message you write.

Purpose

We use the information only to read your request, respond to you, prepare a preliminary conversation and understand whether Soul & Domus may be able to assist with a protected acquisition review or private search.

Legal, Technical And Professional Review

If a real acquisition path begins, additional documents and personal information may be requested and may be shared only with selected professionals involved in the review, such as technicians, lawyers, notaries, tax advisors or other qualified parties, where necessary.

Third-Party Services

This website may load fonts from Google Fonts, link to WhatsApp and use an email delivery service to send contact requests. These third-party services may process data according to their own policies.

Optional Advertising Measurement

If you actively consent through the cookie banner, this website loads the Meta Pixel provided by Meta Platforms Ireland Limited. The Pixel may process online identifiers and information about your visit so that we can measure advertising performance. It is not loaded before your consent, and you may reject optional tracking or reopen your cookie settings at any time.

Retention

We keep communications only for the time reasonably necessary to manage the request, evaluate a possible professional relationship and comply with applicable obligations.

Your Rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your personal information by writing to hello@soulanddomus.com.
